Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains how this personal portfolio website, rahul-kshirsagar.vercel.app, handles information when you visit or contact me.

Website analytics

Vercel Web Analytics provides cookieless statistics about page views, referrers, countries, browsers and devices. Vercel also provides website hosting and may process technical request information to operate and protect the service.

If you choose to accept optional analytics, Google Analytics 4 measures visits and successful contact form submissions without sending the contents of your message. Microsoft Clarity measures interactions, including clicks, scrolling, heatmaps and masked session recordings. These services may use cookies and process device, browser and approximate location information. They are not used here to identify individual visitors or enable personalized advertising.

Your choices

Optional Google Analytics and Clarity scripts are not loaded until you accept analytics. You can decline or change your preference at any time through Privacy Settings in the portfolio footer. Your preference is stored in your browser. Withdrawal stops future optional tracking on this site after the page reloads; it does not automatically erase data previously collected by providers.

Contact enquiries

If you submit the contact form, your name, email address, subject and message are sent through Web3Forms to my email inbox so I can respond to your enquiry. Contact form fields are masked in Clarity recordings. Please do not submit sensitive or confidential information through the form.

Service providers

Information may be processed by the services used to host this website, provide analytics and deliver messages. Their own privacy policies explain their processing and retention practices:

  • Vercel
  • Google
  • Microsoft
  • Web3Forms

Retention and contact

Analytics information is retained according to the relevant provider's settings and policies. Contact enquiries may be kept as needed to respond and maintain correspondence. For questions about this policy or requests concerning your information, email akshirsagar08@gmail.com.
